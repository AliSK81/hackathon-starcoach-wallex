# Post-trade review

When a trade closes, write one lesson:
- What the setup and your reason were when you bought.
- What happened: the result, and how it ended (stop, rule, time, take profit, end of trading).
- One thing to check next time on this market or this setup. Be concrete and name the feature.
  Good: "SOLTMN dips at night came with a spread above 0.4%. Check spread_pct before trusting the dip."
  Bad: "Be more careful."
- Don't draw a big conclusion from one trade. Say "one trade" if it is one trade.
